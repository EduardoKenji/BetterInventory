# v3.0.1 LadyElina777 Auto Crafter audit

Date: 2026-08-26

## Incident

On a level-1 character, Auto Crafter bought more than 130 weapons before the configured 500,000-docket budget was exhausted. The weapons were not visibly upgraded or sacrificed. Opening the weapon inventory afterward produced an out-of-memory crash, leaving manual one-at-a-time sacrifice as the only reported recovery route.

The reporter's console log and exact saved Auto Crafter settings are still required to identify the final backend rejection and the exact allocator failure. The source audit nevertheless found one definite workflow defect and one preventable amplification path.

## Root-cause findings

### 1. Auto Crafter bypassed native progression gates

Darktide's native Path of Trust crafting unlock is level 4 (`pot_crafting = 4`), and the `omnissiah` hub-facility progression record is the native authority when available. Each weapon-family mastery also has an archetype-specific character-level requirement evaluated by `Mastery.is_mastery_unlocked`.

The v3.0.0 controller checked neither gate before issuing backend purchases. This allowed a level-1 character to enter a workflow whose later crafting and mastery operations were not available through the native UI.

v3.0.1 adds both checks before account-operation ownership or the first purchase:

- a known level below 4 blocks every enabled crafting mutation;
- an explicitly locked or missing `omnissiah` record blocks crafting when hub progression is available;
- Psych Ward remains supported when hub progression is genuinely unavailable and the character is at least level 4;
- levels 4–29 require an affirmative selected-family mastery-unlock result before mastery automation starts;
- an explicitly locked selected family is blocked at every character level.

Purchase-only configurations remain available below level 4 because they do not invoke Hadron or mastery mutations.

### 2. Deferred processing explains the apparent inactivity during acquisition

With **Level weapon mastery to 20** and **Defer bad-weapon processing** enabled, non-matching purchases are intentionally collected during the pre-target search. They are upgraded and sacrificed only after an exact target or the closest fallback is selected. Therefore, seeing no upgrades while 130 misses were still being purchased was consistent with the designed phase order.

At an acquisition cap, the default **Use closest fallback candidate** setting should promote the best finite candidate, then process deferred fodder in batches. In the standard single-dump-stat profile, the same projected stat is used for matching and distance, so any processable candidate produces a finite fallback.

Without the reporter's log, the final reason cannot be proven. The remaining plausible branches are:

1. the saved fallback setting was disabled;
2. fallback was selected, but the newly identified level-1 crafting/mastery gate caused the downstream operation to reject;
3. a custom five-stat profile received incomplete projected stats, so every candidate had infinite distance and no valid fallback existed;
4. an optional-mod or backend response differed from the tested contract.

v3.0.1 closes branch 2 before purchases and stops branch 3 after eight consecutive incomplete projections.

### 3. Runaway inventory growth lacked an unconditional cap

The docket cap was enabled by default, but the user-controlled maximum-purchase cap was disabled. Cheap low-level Brunt prices therefore allowed roughly 130 serialized purchases under a 500,000-docket budget.

v3.0.1 adds an independent, non-configurable 40-purchase pre-target safety limit. It applies even when the optional maximum-purchase cap is disabled or configured higher. Forty matches Darktide's own maximum extraction batch size in `CraftingService.extract_weapon_mastery`. Once a target exists, Phase 3 may continue buying fodder, but it processes at most eight pending items per BetterInventory batch, so post-target inventory growth remains bounded rather than accumulating the full mastery requirement.

When fallback is enabled, reaching the safety cap promotes the best finite candidate and continues the normal workflow. Otherwise it stops without another purchase. Users can deliberately start another run; no run silently exceeds 40 pre-target purchases.

### 4. Auto Crafter did not favorite all misses

Rejected and deferred search candidates never call `favorite_item`. Favoriting occurs only after the exact, fallback, first-weapon, or reused inventory candidate is accepted. Existing tests cover rejected misses, authoritative final favoriting, and MyFavorites synchronization; v3.0.1 adds safety-cap and incomplete-projection assertions that favorite calls remain zero.

The 130 weapons therefore were not all favorited by Auto Crafter. A log or save-state inspection would be needed to prove whether another mod changed their favorite state.

### 5. The inventory crash is heap pressure, not a demonstrated infinite BetterInventory leak

Darktide's `ViewElementGrid` constructs one widget and alignment widget for every layout entry when `present_grid_layout` runs. The native weapon inventory passes its complete item layout to that grid. A large inventory therefore creates a large one-time widget/icon allocation before scrolling can limit what is visible.

BetterInventory passes the same layout through once and does not append a duplicate weapon entry. Its projected-stat and sort caches use weak keys and are explicitly replaced or cleared at view teardown. Auto Crafter's raw gear cache and summarized snapshot are also cleared after a settled Brunt view closes; an active background workflow may retain them until it settles because crafting validation still needs authoritative items, but that ownership is bounded and has terminal cleanup.

The backend harness summarizes 1,027 raw records into exactly 1,026 active-character entries, including one fail-closed malformed entry, proving linear cardinality rather than exponential duplication. The lifecycle suite verifies normal and abnormal inventory, vendor, Character Overview, and Auto Crafter teardown paths.

Fatshark has separately acknowledged out-of-memory reports involving inventory/crafting, and public crash logs show the fixed Lua heap reaching its reserved limit while loading inventories containing hundreds of weapons. LadyElina777's 130 new weapons—on top of the character's existing gear—could therefore push an already large layout past the engine's available Lua heap. BetterInventory may increase each card's presentation cost when detailed layouts are enabled, but no unbounded loop, recursive grid rebuild, duplicate layout expansion, or retained per-item registry was found in this audit.

Relevant public reports:

- <https://forums.fatsharkgames.com/t/out-of-memory-crash/99211>
- <https://forums.fatsharkgames.com/t/known-issue-out-of-memory-crashes/87186/47>

The v3.0.1 fix is preventive: do not create an unbounded additional inventory in the first place. It does not claim to repair Darktide's allocator or make every already-oversized inventory renderable.

## Source contracts checked

- `scripts/settings/player/player_progression_unlocks.lua`: Hadron Path of Trust level 4 and `omnissiah` facility identity.
- `scripts/settings/narrative/narrative_stories.lua`: native hub-facility progression predicate.
- `scripts/utilities/mastery.lua`: archetype/family character-level unlock predicate.
- `scripts/managers/data_service/services/crafting_service.lua`: extraction is serialized into batches of at most 40 gear IDs.
- `scripts/ui/view_elements/view_element_grid/view_element_grid.lua`: every layout entry creates a widget during presentation.
- `scripts/ui/views/inventory_weapons_view/inventory_weapons_view.lua`: the complete sorted item layout is presented to the native grid.

## Regression boundaries

- Level 1 with crafting enabled: blocked before purchase.
- Level 10 with no hub progression data: allowed only after native selected-family mastery discovery confirms unlock.
- Explicitly locked/missing `omnissiah`: blocked.
- Level 30: existing workflow remains available; explicit family lock still blocks.
- Purchase-only low-level configuration: remains allowed.
- Optional purchase cap disabled and large docket budget: cannot dispatch purchase 41 before a target.
- Eight consecutive incomplete custom-stat projections: stops; no invalid fallback or favorite.
- Finite closest fallback: existing exact distance and Phase 3 behavior preserved.
- Deferred post-target fodder: existing eight-item batches and single ordered upgrade lane preserved.
- Large backend gear response: exact linear item cardinality and fail-closed malformed entries preserved.
